# Overnight research sweep — 2026-08-21

(6 parallel search agents + synthesis; arxiv IDs NOT yet independently verified — check before citing.)

(1) TOP 10 PAPERS BY RELEVANCE

1. IntervenGen (arXiv 2405.01472, IROS 2024) — Same robosuite/MimicGen stack; its ablation (10 interventions + 1000 replayed recovery expansions = 70% vs 10 demos + 1000 full-demo expansions = 16%) shows that changing the START-STATE distribution of prescribed demos, not the instance subset, is worth +54pp — the one published axis orthogonal to our replicated "any subset loses to uniform" null.
2. Best-Arm ID with LLM Judges and Limited Audits (arXiv 2601.21471) — The complete algorithmic package for our regime: unknown-bias cheap proxy on every pull, rationed ground-truth audits with Neyman allocation and IPW residual correction, plus a proof that infinite proxy data cannot fix mis-selection without audits — the formal version of our measured Goodhart.
3. CUPID (arXiv 2506.19121, CoRL 2025) — Influence on closed-loop RETURN (not loss) via rollout-weighted REINFORCE, exactly matching our gradient-encoding principle's prediction of what should work; our 450-rollout archive lets us test it at higher power than the paper's own m=25-100 ablation.
4. PROBE (arXiv 2607.06879) — One-sided exact-chi-square residual-variance certificate for proxies of unknown correlation; by construction it makes the noise-variance-subtraction pathology that broke our calibrated-validity gate impossible.
5. Critical-Interval MSE (arXiv 2606.29898) — Mechanistic explanation of our Goodhart: raw action-MSE correlates the WRONG WAY (+0.67) because transit motion dominates, and restricting to task-critical windows flips it to -0.98 — directly testable with our grasp-window detector against the archived poisoned set.
6. SureSim (arXiv 2510.04354) — The closest published instantiation of our plan (cheap fidelity + paired ground-truth PPI rectification, DP and pi0); its honest 20-25% gain is the realistic ceiling prior for any surrogate, and it is the template for the G1 sim2real chapter.
7. No Free Lunch for PPI (arXiv 2505.20178) — The pre-registerable kill numbers for the DP surrogate: break-even at rho ~ 1/sqrt(n-2), but the variance cut is (1-rho^2), so at our n and 3.2pp floor anything below arm-level rho 0.5 cannot beat buying more seeds.
8. Counterfactual Action Sensitivity Coverage (arXiv 2607.27261) — The only selection method with a matched-budget win over random, and it selects in policy-RESPONSE space, not input-region space (the space all our null arms lived in); honestly reports random closing the gap at large budgets.
9. pi*0.6 / RECAP (arXiv 2511.14759, Physical Intelligence) — The pi0 authors' own answer to pi0's residual failures is correction data plus advantage weighting, not more expert demos and not loss proxies — independent industry alignment with our facts (2) and (3).
10. Data Scaling Laws in IL (arXiv 2410.18647, ICLR 2025) — Marginal value of demos concentrated anywhere saturates past ~50/environment; the published backbone that makes our uniform-beats-subsets replications the EXPECTED regime, not a failed method.

Honorable mentions used below: Rewind-IL (2604.16683, conformal failure detection + respawn probe), RINSE (2604.23000) and SCIZOR (2505.22626) as zero-rollout quality signals, decoupled surrogate bandits (2607.09015) as an architecture rule, N-SCORE (2603.13616) for anytime-valid stopping.

(2) TOP 5 RUNNABLE EXPERIMENTS BY INFORMATION PER GPU-HOUR

1. Certified-validity gate v2 + negative-control battery (PROBE-style), zero GPU, ~2 days analysis. Rebuild the gate as a one-sided upper confidence bound on OLS residual variance (exact chi-square, df=n-2) using ONLY the pre-registered paired-seed pulls from the ledger (avoids adaptive-collection bias), and run every candidate cheap signal through it: loss composite (known-bad control), pure noise, true delta + synthetic noise at rho in {0.3,0.5,0.7,0.9}, CI-MSE with our grasp-window restriction, RINSE SAL/TED, the gradient-quality score. Pre-registered success: gate stays closed on the poisoned/noise controls in >=95% of bootstrap replays, opens with power >=0.8 on synthetic rho>=0.7, and the CI-MSE variant demotes the poisoned dataset out of the top half. This yields the calibration curve (usable-rho vs n at sigma 3.2pp) that no competitor publishes, and it is the placebo/attainability machinery FSC lacks.

2. Frozen-policy respawn attainability probe (Rewind-IL pattern), ~0.2 pull-equivalent (2 x 450 rollouts, no training), ~1 day harness work. On detected failure (never-touched by step T), rewind the robot to the last safe state and let frozen pi0 retry up to 2x; measure recovered success mass paired against standard eval (rollout floor 0.88pp makes ~2pp readable). Pre-registered gate: recovered mass >=8pp of the 42pp failure mass funds the recovery-data arm (experiment 5); <3pp pre-registers recovery data as ceiling-limited and redirects that budget. This is probe-then-allocate applied to our own next experiment.

3. Lite-pull fidelity with MEASURED bias bound, ~0 GPU for the analysis + roughly half a race for confirmation. From archived checkpoint pairs, compute the upper CI on |SR_10k - SR_20k| (noise-floor anatomy already says ~0 bias, 2.4pp choice term) and the variance cost of 150 vs 450 rollouts; if xi_upper <= 2pp, adopt screen-with-lite-pulls / confirm-with-full-pulls (audit-BAI structure), confirmed by one race where lite-pull arm ranking vs full-pull ranking has Spearman >=0.8. Success buys a 2-3x cost cut on every future race with zero Goodhart surface — it is the same measurement, truncated. Fold in N-SCORE sequential stopping (backtest on archived episode streams first) to reinvest saved rollouts into seeds, the dominant noise term.

4. DP-surrogate concordance with pre-registered NFL kill, ~1-2 H100-days, zero new pi0 pulls. Finetune DP on 6-8 archived candidate mixtures that already have pi0 ground truth, same paired-eval inits; compute arm-level rho(DP delta, pi0 delta), also on logit scale (DP baseline ~10% compresses deltas). Pre-registered reads: rho>=0.7 adopt DP as low fidelity with PPI rectification (halves pull cost); 0.5-0.7 use only for Neyman audit allocation; <0.5 kill per the No-Free-Lunch threshold. Add CUPID-style influence-on-return scoring from the same DP gradients + archived rollouts as a rider — a null there at 450-rollout power is stronger than CUPID's own ablation could detect.

5. IntervenGen recovery arm race (gated by experiment 2), 4-6 pulls (2-3 paired seeds x 2 arms), ~2-3 days engineering. Harvest first-failure states from archived rollout hdf5s (never-closed-gripper heuristic covers the 76% mode), rewind K steps, MimicGen SE(3) segment replay to generate 200 recovery demos; race vs 200 uniform-diverse at paired seeds on the existing bandit_v1 runner. Pre-registered read: recovery - uniform >= +6.5pp (2-sigma at sigma 3.2pp, 2 seeds) confirms the one prescription axis that beats uniform; a null extends the subset-restriction law across data TYPES at a strong-policy operating point (58% base) where IntervenGen never tested — publishable either way, and the experiment-2 probe doubles as its placebo control.

(3) FRANK NOVELTY ASSESSMENT

The crowded claim — "our selection signal beats random" — is not winnable and our own data says it is mostly false at honest power (Chasing Random, the scaling-law saturation regime, our two-task replication). The strongest remaining ICRA slot is the one nobody in either adjacent literature occupies: a CERTIFIED prescription pipeline, i.e., probe-then-allocate where every cheap signal must pass a one-sided validity certificate calibrated at the measured noise floor, with real negative controls, before it is allowed to spend budget. The MF-bandit line explicitly leaves online bias estimation open (TACC states it as future work), the robot-curation line (FSC, CUPID, DataMIL, SCIZOR) publishes no placebo or attainability gate at all, and SureSim certifies fidelities for evaluation but not for allocation. We hold assets nobody else has: a measured Goodhart positive control (poisoned set ranked #1 by loss), a decomposed noise floor, replicated nulls at known power, and a ledger of ground-truth pulls to calibrate against. The paper is therefore: the certified validity region for cheap prescription signals (where every published proxy actually lands on it), plus the boundary map of prescription itself — instance-subset selection provably dead, execution quality alive, recovery-type data decided by the gated IntervenGen race — with the G1 pipeline as the transfer argument. Two risks to state plainly: if the recovery arm wins big, reviewers may read it as an IntervenGen replication, so the contribution must be the strong-policy regime (58% base, compressed effects) and the noise-floor-honest, placebo-controlled protocol, not the axis itself; and the certificate contribution is only as strong as its negative controls, so the battery in experiment 1 is not optional garnish — it is the figure that separates this from FSC and SureSim.
